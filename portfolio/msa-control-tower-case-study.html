Portfolio Case Study

MSA Monetization & Publisher Strategy Control Tower

A unified control tower that connects intelligence, AI-driven decisioning, publisher strategy, sales, governance & executive visibility into a single operating system for ads monetization.

Role
Executive Strategy & Systems Architect
Focus Areas
Agentic AI, Monetization Strategy, Cross-Publisher Yield, Governance
Environment
Multi-surface ads ecosystem (MSN, Outlook, Xbox, ABK, MAI)

Problem

Monetization, publisher strategy, and AI experimentation were happening in silos. Intelligence lived in one place, sales in another, policy in another, and executives lacked a single view of performance, risk, and opportunity across surfaces and publishers.

Solution

I designed the MSA Monetization & Publisher Strategy Control Tower as a layered system that:

  • Unifies signals across publishers, surfaces and demand into a single intelligence layer.
  • Uses AI-driven decisioning to optimize pricing, packaging & surface prioritization.
  • Aligns publisher strategy, GTM, sales and partner motions on shared revenue plays.
  • Embeds responsible AI guardrails, policy alignment & trust-by-design controls.
  • Gives executives a live view of revenue, yield, ROAS and risk across the portfolio.

My Contributions

  • Architected the multi-layer control tower model (intelligence → decisioning → publisher strategy → GTM → governance → executive views).
  • Defined data flows, cross-team interfaces and decision rights between product, sales, publishers, and AI systems.
  • Mapped monetization & publisher outcomes to executive dashboards and scenario modeling.
  • Embedded responsible AI & governance checkpoints into the operating rhythm, not just as an afterthought.
Illustrative Impact
• +24% modeled revenue uplift from unified monetization strategy.
• Higher publisher yield & fill rate through cross-surface optimization.
• Reduced execution friction by giving leaders & teams one shared source of truth.
